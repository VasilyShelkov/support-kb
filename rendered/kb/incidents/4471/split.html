What each problem is

parser (ticket). The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first books 7 April and gets 4 July. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so the reports come from all of them: through the app, by email and through the booking-v2 form.

hint (ticket). The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the day first. The symptom is the same swap, but it is a different bug: a fix to the parser leaves the hint wrong. Only tenants on booking-v3 have it, and only for bookings made through that form.

one-day (ticket). Dates that move by one day: a Tuesday shown as the Monday before, an invite that says one day while the calendar says the day before, a client in Toronto who gets a different day than a client in Lisbon for the same meeting. A date one day off is not a swapped month. These are the reports the desk already read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The desk files them under parser by tenant and counts them there, so they count under both.

How the reports are counted

The counts are the desk’s own: the cause table on #4471, as exported.

  • One report per id. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • The cause comes from the tenant: the locale it writes dates in and the booking form it runs. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, so it is not used.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move stays counted where the desk files it, under its tenant’s cause, and is counted again under one-day.

Lessons

  • Read what a page shows, not only what it says. The #4471 page said one issue; its own table showed more than one.
  • The subjects in #4471 all say “wrong day”. The tenant, the way the report came in and the body say which problem it is.
